# Deck author

Turn the approved staging.yaml into slides. Keep each headline and its place in the argument. For a live talk, storyboard what the audience will see while the speaker delivers each beat. Use the project's brand contract or [the unbranded starter](assets/deck.html).

Follow [the deck authoring workflow](workflow.md) for composition, diagrams, typography, motion, structural checks, contact sheets and PDF export. Tool paths in the workflow start here. Inspect every slide and the exported pages before delivery.
