### Changed
- desk-tools: role credentials now come from one internal provider (`tools/desk/internal/credential`), with the `desktoken` CLI and `deskkit.RoleTokenForOwner` as its two adapters. A desk verb that looks up a role token (`deskboard`, `deskflip`, `deskdispatch`, `deskread`, `deskwt`, `scanloop`, `deskgit`, `cellctl`) no longer forks `desktoken` from `PATH`. It mints in process with the same guard, audit row, cache and refusals, and works on a host where `desktoken` is not installed. A refused identity has no fallback.
- `desktoken`: a mint records the forge's own `expires_at` beside the cache (`<cache>.expires`). A cache is never reused past that recorded expiry. A cache from before this change is held to the conservative reuse bound, mtime + 50 minutes. `--ttl` measures to that expiry and adds `expiry=recorded` or `expiry=bound` to its line. An empty cache file is replaced by a mint, and `<PREFIX>_INSTALL_ID` must be digits only. `--fresh`, the GitLab rotation lock and `--no-rotate` behave as before.
