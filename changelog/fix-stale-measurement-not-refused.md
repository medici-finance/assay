### Fixed
- Managed Go caches: a recorded measurement that is only stale (older than five minutes but stamped, owned, clean and unchanged) no longer refuses bounded launch preparation, final consumer enrollment or `cellctl cache-run`. They admit on it, print `cache-enrollment: recorded measurement is stale: <age> old …` on stderr, and report `"admission":"precheck-stale"` with the measurement's own time. An unstamped, future-dated or missing measurement still refuses as before. A launch owner that wants a freshness bound sets `measurement_max_age_seconds` in the cache policy: preparation and enrollment then refuse a measurement older than it, and one clean clears that refusal. `cellctl up` no longer runs a clean for age alone.
