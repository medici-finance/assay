### Fixed
- `statusgen`'s acceptance-transition chain no longer leaves a delivered brief permanently unamendable after a main-side edit that was later restored. A span that leaves the accepted Verify table and returns to it before the next link, with the Verify section byte-identical after CRLF to LF line-ending normalisation and `version:` equal, is now a no-op: the next link keeps the accepted revision as its predecessor. Any other unlinked change still refuses, including a restore with reordered rows, any other whitespace difference or a different version, and a round trip after the final link. `docs/protected-paths.md` and the `R-original-table-closure` contract row state the exception.
