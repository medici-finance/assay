### Changed
- The CLI migration registry gains an `exempt` row state: an entrypoint whose parser port was withdrawn because its domain retires keeps its current parser until retired, needs a reason and an existing owner brief, and is not wired to the completion gate. The routing check refuses an exempt row without a reason or owner.
- Board: the desktools-v2 per-verb Cobra/Viper ports of verbs the target architecture retires or re-homes are withdrawn; Cobra/Viper is now a stated requirement for every new command and verb adapter, and desktools-v2/17 enforces it on every surviving CLI. The deskdispatch port (desktools-v2/21) stays, since it has merged. graph-execution/08 now defines the gate-red class contract that auto-triage/01–03 implement, and the approval-carry rule (desk-supervision/25) is re-homed in the work/policy evaluator.
