### Fixed
- `statusgen --scan-issues` and `--transcribe-scan` now re-derive a reactivated placeholder's `gate:` and `labels:` from the live issue when it reopens (or loses an excluded label), instead of carrying the retired file's stale frontmatter forward — a reopened issue that gained a risk label now comes back `gate: human` rather than staying `gate: model`. A stored human gate (quoted, commented or implied by stored risk labels, as the reader decodes it) is never relaxed on reactivation, a `labels:` flow list wrapped over two lines is replaced whole, the dry run prints the gate a reactivation will write, and a reactivate built without the live issue is refused.
