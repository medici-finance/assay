### Fixed
- `verifyflip` provenance no longer refuses a flip because of a criss-cross merge of main into a branch when the branch side made no net change to the Evidence section. Before this, a merge was trivial only when the other parent's Evidence matched the Evidence at every merge base, so a branch unchanged since the newer merge base but different from the older one was judged as having written Evidence, and the flip was refused under the merger's identity. Now the other parent counts as bringing nothing when its Evidence matches the Evidence at some merge base and the kept parent's Evidence matches none of them. A merge that drops a later verdict added on the other side, including one in a criss-cross history, is still judged.
