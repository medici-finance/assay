### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` and the verify-held scan no longer refuse a strict `VERIFY: PASS` over prose that only mentions a hold state: a prose line outside a results table counts only where it records a structured hold signal (an upper-case HELD / COULD-NOT-CHECK / COULD-NOT-RUN marker, a RISK-VALUE record, a BLOCKED or FAIL verdict, a named Verify row, a stated reason or value, a line-leading state word, "held at" a status, or a non-zero count). Zero tallies such as `could-not-check 0`, sentences describing what a mutation does, recounted past holds, and RISK-VALUE prose mapping an outcome to could-not-check no longer hold the flip. Table rows are read exactly as before.
