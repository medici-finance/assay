### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` and the verify-held scan no longer refuse a strict `VERIFY: PASS` over three prose shapes that only recount a state word. Each is excused on the lower-case occurrence alone, only on a line outside every table, and only in the context that makes it a recount:
  - a zero tally that closes its clause, with no row named before it and no reason the run did not happen (the list below) in its sentence (`obligations 0; could-not-check 0.`);
  - `the mutation rounds could-not-check to pass`, whose subject is the mutation itself, on a line that names no row, or the same words as one parenthesised item of a mutation list (`... named mutation ...: row 2 (new adapter rounds could-not-check to pass)`) whose subject is one to three lower-case words that do not open on an article or determiner (`the`, `a`, `an`, `this`, `that`, `these`, `those`, `my`, `your`, `his`, `her`, `its`, `our`, `their`, `whose`, `which`, `what`, `some`, `any`, `each`, `every`, `all`, `both`, `either`, `neither`, `no`, `another`) and name no word for the run or its tools (`verifyrun`, `verifier`, `gate`, `runner`, `check` and the like), on a line that gives no reason the run did not happen (offline, absent, down, skipped, unavailable, unreachable, never, not, cannot, a contracted negation such as `didn't`, without, no runner, cluster, host, mutation or mutant);
  - a flip hold named as an earlier entry's (`the earlier flip was held on #4536.`, with `the` directly before `earlier`, or `its board flip` in a clause that opens on the earlier PASS or entry and sets nothing against it), whose tracker ends the line or is followed only by `This entry is a fresh run.`, with no row named before it, no reason a run did not happen (the list above) and no verified flip or anything still in force on the line.

  A row is any row word or R and a digit (`row 3`, `Row3`, `row three`, `R3`), inline code included. Any other verb, tense or target, a subject outside parentheses other than the mutation, a parenthesis subject that is the run or its tools, a flip that is not named as the earlier entry's (`the flip`, `this entry's flip`, `this entry's earlier flip`, `its earlier flip`), `its flip` beside `unlike`, `compared`, `superseded` or `this entry`, a flip hold followed by a reason or any other sentence, a labelled disposition, a capital or mixed-case marker, a zero followed by a noun, a pipe or HTML table line, and any line between an HTML table's open and close tags (only a `</table` followed by a space, `/`, `>` or the line end closes it: not `</table-x>`, and not one quoted in code, a comment or a start tag's attribute value, nor one made by joining the text either side of a code span or comment) are read as before.

  Residual: the excuse reads wording, not intent. A mutation sentence giving a no-run reason in words outside the list above (`the runner timed out`), a parenthesised mutation item whose subject is a run word outside the list (`deskverify rounds could-not-check to pass`), `its flip` after `the earlier entry passed, but` where `its` means this entry, or an earlier entry's flip hold that still applies but is not said to (`still`, `remains`, `continues`), is excused.
