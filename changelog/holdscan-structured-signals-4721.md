### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` and the verify-held scan no longer refuse a strict `VERIFY: PASS` over three prose shapes that only recount a state word. Each is excused on the lower-case occurrence alone, only on a line outside every table, and only in the context that makes it a recount:
  - a zero tally that closes its clause, with no row named before it (`obligations 0; could-not-check 0.`);
  - `the mutation rounds could-not-check to pass`, whose subject is the mutation itself, on a line that names no row, or the same words as one parenthesised item of a mutation list (`... named mutation ...: row 2 (new adapter rounds could-not-check to pass)`), on a line that gives no reason the run did not happen (offline, absent, down, skipped, unavailable, unreachable, never, not, without, no runner, cluster, host, mutation or mutant);
  - a flip hold named as an earlier entry's (`the earlier flip was held on #4536.`, or `its board flip` in a clause that names the earlier PASS or entry), whose tracker ends the sentence, with no row named before it and no verified flip or anything still in force on the line.

  A row is any row word or R and a digit (`row 3`, `Row3`, `row three`, `R3`), inline code included. Any other verb, tense, subject or target, a flip that is not named as an earlier entry's (`the flip`, `this entry's flip`), a flip hold followed by a reason or any other sentence, a labelled disposition, a capital or mixed-case marker, a zero followed by a noun, a pipe or HTML table line, and any line between an HTML table's open and close tags are read as before.

  Residual: the excuse reads wording, not intent. A mutation sentence giving a no-run reason in words outside the list above (`the runner timed out`), or an earlier entry's flip hold that still applies but is not said to (`still`, `remains`, `continues`), is excused.
