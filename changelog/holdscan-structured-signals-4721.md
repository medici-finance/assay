### Changed
- The verify-held scan behind `statusgen verifyflip`, the verify-gate card and the close-verify check now reads only witness rows and lines that state a current hold (#4721, ruling A). Table lines are read as before. On any other line, `HELD` or `could-not-check` counts only as one of three signals: a marker, meaning the word is not written entirely in lower case (`HELD`, `Held`, `Could-Not-Check`); a row result, meaning the line names a row (`row 3`, `Row3`, `R3`), at any case; or a verdict line (`VERIFY:` or `RISK-VALUE:`), at any case. Lower-case `held` or `could-not-check` in any other prose is no longer a hold. That covers a recounted tally, a mutation sentence, a past flip and a step said to be held. The witness row carries its own state.
