### Fixed
- deskdispatch: the phantom check reads the delivering PRs as a bounded window of the most recent open and merged changes. When that window is incomplete and does not represent the item's brief, the brief is now looked up by its `Brief:` trailer (and, under `--cross-repo`, its `Cross-repo-brief:` value) through the forge's search. A merged or open hit refuses the fresh dispatch as already represented. A failed or partial lookup, or a forge backend without the search, holds the dispatch as could-not-check (exit 6) instead of treating the brief as undelivered. A complete window makes no lookup.
