### Fixed
- The forge-local authority channels now free a connection's slot before closing it. A client's call ends at that close, so a back-to-back operator call on the one-slot private control channel is no longer refused as busy by the caller's own just-finished call. This was the cause of the intermittent `TestLocalAuthorityPrivateControlProtocol` failure ("data channel modified authority"): the second readback had been refused, and the authority state had not changed. The test now reports a refused readback separately from a changed one, and names the receipt fields that differ.
