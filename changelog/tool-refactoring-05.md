- Verification outcomes now have one shared protocol in `statusgen/evaluation`: the outcome words, the reduction of `rows`, `row_results`, `rows_total` and `rows_passed`, and the per-file and legacy record codec (its own subpackage, `statusgen/evaluation/outcomerecord`, so the library itself stays free of file, JSON and clock imports) are defined once, and statusgen and desk-tools call it instead of keeping copies.
- `deskevidence --outcome-record` now refuses a `verified` record that holds or fails rows. The outcome-word checks released earlier (an absent or unrecognised `outcome` is refused, and `Verified` or ` verified ` gets the closure check) are unchanged; they now call the shared protocol.
- A class guard (`TestOutcomeProtocolClassGuard`) fails if a second copy of the outcome vocabulary, the exact-spelling comparison, the brief-key grammar or the row-result words appears outside the shared package.
