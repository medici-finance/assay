- Verification outcomes now have one shared protocol in `statusgen/evaluation`: the outcome words, the reduction of `rows`, `row_results`, `rows_total` and `rows_passed`, and the per-file and legacy record codec are defined once, and statusgen and desk-tools call it instead of keeping copies.
- `deskevidence --outcome-record` refuses a record that names no `outcome` or spells it other than a canonical word, and refuses a `verified` record that holds or fails rows; the closure guard and the sidecar gate treat `Verified` and ` verified ` as the pass a reader counts.
- A class guard (`TestOutcomeProtocolClassGuard`) fails if a second copy of the outcome vocabulary, the exact-spelling comparison, the brief-key grammar or the row-result words appears outside the shared package.
