### Fixed
- `deskevidence` now requires the Evidence section a landing adds to be one a Markdown renderer and the lint read the same way, before it checks or stamps any on-behalf-of principal. Added lines must be blank lines, `|`-led rows under a header with a delimiter row a renderer accepts, or note lines that show no App-run or on-behalf-of text. Only the cell the lint reads as Runner may show as an App-run Runner cell. A Runner cell that shows as App-run once its HTML, entity, emphasis, code-span or escape markup is removed, its link, image or autolink syntax is read as the text a renderer shows, or a closed comment is hidden, must also read as App-run to the lint, and an App-run Runner cell may not hold link, image or autolink syntax. A bracketed `[text]` that opens no link is also read as a shortcut reference link, and a cell that opens with an App login followed by `[bot]` is read as App-run whatever follows it and whatever invisible, space-like, fullwidth or lookalike characters it holds, so a Runner, headerless, outside-section or no-section cell that a reader sees as App-run is refused wherever it stands. Cells are located at the same bytes in the landed line whatever characters an HTML comment on that line holds. Rows added directly after a note line land when they are exactly as wide as the table's header and its Runner column is the last, since both readings then take the same Runner cell. Headings shown as Evidence outside the one section the lint reads (ATX, setext or HTML, with entities decoded and link syntax read as its text) take no new rows. Rows outside that section may not show as App-run. The checks are rules held by the tool rather than a full Markdown parse, and the lint-way read of the landed section stays the last check. Nothing is written when a landing is refused.
- The `--brief-path` merge now puts a blank line before an appended block that opens with a table header, so a witness table appended after an attestation line starts its own table.
