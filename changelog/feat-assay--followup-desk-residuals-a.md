### Fixed
- `deskevidence` now requires the Evidence section a landing adds to be one a Markdown renderer and the lint read the same way, before it checks or stamps any on-behalf-of principal. Inside a table, added lines must be `|`-led rows under a header with a delimiter row a renderer accepts, and a line that closes a table without a blank line must show no App-run or on-behalf-of text; a line after a blank line or a heading starts no table and is not read for that text. Only the cell the lint reads as Runner may show as an App-run Runner cell. A Runner cell that shows as App-run once its HTML, entity, emphasis, code-span or escape markup is removed, its link, image or autolink syntax is read as the text a renderer shows, or a closed comment is hidden, must also read as App-run to the lint, and an App-run Runner cell may not hold link, image or autolink syntax. A bracketed `[text]` that opens no link is also read as a shortcut reference link, and a cell is read for a login and tag by its structure rather than by folding the characters it knows: when its text up to the lint's cut (extended over spaces inside a tag) holds a bracketed tag of exactly three letters or digits, as `[bot]` does, or a wider tag beside any character outside the ASCII login alphabet, it is read as App-run, so a character in that text that no rule names fails closed, while text whose letters are all CJK, such as `【東京都】`, is not read as a login. Each reading is also read with every character that renders as nothing (marks, default-ignorable, control, format, private-use and unassigned code points, and the blank braille pattern, raw or as an entity) dropped, and with every leading word that holds no ASCII letter or digit skipped, whatever its characters are and whether or not they render, since such a word cannot be a login (the leading words are also read as a renderer shows them, each by itself, a word it replaces with a glyph, such as `:name:`, read as one symbol, while the rest of the text stays as written; a span it can typeset as math, paired from any two runs of `$`, is read beside the text as written, both dropped and as its text read from each place a control word, a brace or white space ends, with its markup removed; the text after a line break or block element tag is read as a line of its own; the text of a raw HTML element a sanitizer can drop, and apart from it the text of an element a renderer hides by default, such as `<rp>` or one with a `hidden` attribute, is read both kept and dropped; and every trim takes any Unicode white space), so that text starts at the word a reader takes for the name, and a word in parentheses on a line that closes a table is read too. So a Runner, headerless, outside-section or no-section cell that shows as App-run under any of these readings is refused. Cells are located at the same bytes in the landed line whatever characters an HTML comment on that line holds. Rows added directly after a note line land when they are exactly as wide as the table's header and its Runner column is the last, since both readings then take the same Runner cell. Headings shown as Evidence outside the one section the lint reads (ATX, setext or HTML, with entities decoded and link syntax read as its text) take no new rows. Rows outside that section may not show as App-run. The checks are rules held by the tool rather than a full Markdown parse; each reading only adds to the others, so a later dollar sign or glyph can add a reading but never remove one, and the lint-way read of the landed section stays the last check. Nothing is written when a landing is refused.
- The `--brief-path` merge now puts a blank line before an appended block that opens with a table header, so a witness table appended after an attestation line starts its own table.
