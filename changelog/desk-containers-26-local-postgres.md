### Added
- Local PostgreSQL for a multi-cell host (`containers/postgres/`, `docs/postgres.md`): its own `assay-postgres` compose project with a digest-pinned `postgres:18.6-trixie` image on loopback only, a runtime-only superuser secret, and peer/SCRAM authentication in place of the image's default `trust`. `provision-cell.sh` gives each cell one database and one role without superuser, database-creation or role-creation rights, sending only a client-computed SCRAM verifier. `backup.sh` writes per-cell dumps with a manifest and retention; `restore.sh` restores one cell through a one-shot non-superuser role and leaves the cell `NOLOGIN` until `--enable-login`; `check-isolation.sh` checks the database-layer controls; `drill.sh` proves the whole flow on throwaway servers. The doc states the boundary: the database layer separates cell roles, while host cells on one OS user are not separated at the credential layer and can reach the superuser.
- `cellctl`: two `cell.env` keys, `CELL_DB_DSN` and `CELL_DB_PASSFILE`. With the DSN set, every role window gets `ASSAY_CELL_DB_DSN` and `PGPASSFILE` (host launch, dry-run plan, scrubbed environment, Codex forwarding), and `cellctl check` reports the DSN carries no password, the passfile is absolute, readable and 0600/0400, and that container cells cannot reach the database yet. A DSN carrying a password is never composed and `desk` refuses the launch; a key a cell does not compose is removed from the launch environment, never inherited. Unset, nothing changes.
- `cellctl`: a Kubernetes compatibility cell that sets `CELL_DB_DSN` or `CELL_DB_PASSFILE` is refused by `desk`, `up` and `check`, naming the key, instead of silently dropping it.
