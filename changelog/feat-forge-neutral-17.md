### Added
- `deskrun log` prints a run's log once per job (every job of a GitHub run or GitLab pipeline; a job over the 4 MiB cap keeps its true last 4 MiB, and a log past the read, job-count or archive bounds is could-not-check rather than a shortened log; terminal control sequences stripped) under the calling worker or reviewer role's own read token, without reaching the write verbs' per-repo budget or circuit breaker (reads record under their own audit key, `deskrun-read`), and `deskrun retry` re-runs only the failed jobs of one run under the roster-bound run credential, refusing a human-bound repo before any mint. Both ride two new forge operations, `RunLog` and `RetryRun`, on the GitHub and GitLab backends.
