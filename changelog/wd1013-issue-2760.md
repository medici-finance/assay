### Fixed
- `deskdispatch` keeps the authoring stamp and the review stamp apart. Before, both wrote `dispatched-model:` / `dispatched-tier:`, so whichever ran later replaced the other. A worker-kit stamp, from `--stamp-only` or from a worker dispatch with `--pr`, now writes `authored-model:` / `authored-tier:` and never reads, removes or writes a `dispatched-*` label. A `--kit review` stamp still writes `dispatched-*`, and it is the only pair the capability floor and the auto-lane read. An authoring stamp therefore no longer satisfies a review floor.
