### Fixed
- The nine desk write verbs (`deskclose`, `deskdisposition`, `deskevidence`, `deskfile`, `desklabel`, `deskpathguard`, `deskpr`, `deskreply`, `deskrestamp`) now read their role credential in process and read-only, through `deskkit.WriteVerbRoleCredential`. They used to fork `desktoken <role> --repo <slug>` without `--forge`, and on a GitLab-bound repo that shape rotated the role's PAT as a side effect of an ordinary close, label or reply. A GitLab repo with no provisioned custody file is now refused (exit 5); it never triggers a rotation. GitHub behaviour is unchanged.
