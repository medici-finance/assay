### Added
- `brief-flow-event/v1` gains two record kinds beside the milestone fact: `wait`, one receipt for a named wait between pipeline stages (intake to dispatch, dispatch to worker, worker to review, review to verify, finding to remediation), and `human_intervention`, one evidenced human act with a category, a taxonomy version, explicit brief links or unallocated scope, and roles only. Facts written before this change are still valid and keep their digests. A milestone-fact reader refuses the new kinds instead of misreading them. The milestones are unchanged.
- An optional `context` member links an observation to its purpose, policy revision, harness, build, requested, resolved and served model, finding and fix, and external quality, cost or adoption sources. It is an observation member, so it never changes a fact's digest, and every part it leaves out stays `unknown`.
- Intervention counts deduplicate on act id: a retry or a mirrored copy adds zero, and an act linked to several briefs counts once in the fleet total. An act counts as precise only when the source itself authenticated a human initiator. On-behalf-of app metadata, unknown attribution and `unclassified` acts are counted as unqualified. When source coverage is unknown, every count is unknown rather than zero.
- Two projection profiles: an operator-only restricted profile, which is refused inside a git work tree, and a committed profile built by allowlist copy and checked against that allowlist. Intervention counts in the committed profile are held unless a recorded publication policy covers the source, and its `policy_revision` names that policy. Wait statistics still render.
- The board historian and its replayer refuse human-act lines on read and on write.
- New acceptance runner `bash tests/brief-flow/17.sh <mode>`, with synthetic fixtures under `statusgen/testdata/brief-flow/interventions/`.
