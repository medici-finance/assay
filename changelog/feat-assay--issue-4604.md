### Fixed
- `statusgen`'s board-honesty planner now asks the reconcile's own lint hold whether it would write each `already-merged-unflipped` row. A row the hold refuses is labelled `flip held: <problem>` with the same lint problem the reconcile reports, and its fix tells you to cite the design record (`design:`, §4.4) or record the ruling (`ruling:`, §4.5), instead of "reconcile the row". The row is still held out of dispatch, and the board lists it on its own line.
- A row the reconcile could not evaluate at all (the evaluation failed, it found no todo row to write, or the lint has no stream model for the row) is labelled `flip hold could-not-check: <reason>` and rendered on its own board line with a fix that says to re-run the reconcile, not to cite a design or a ruling. It is never shown as a definite hold.
- `statusgen` evaluates the reconcile's hold once per invocation and shares the verdicts between the notice and the dispatch hold, so `--lint` no longer pays for the whole-board evaluation twice.
