### Fixed
- `deskdispatch --kit verifier` now has one dispatch shape for a cross-repository tracking brief (the brief in one repo, its deliverable in another) that claims, verifies and lands Evidence: `--repo <deliverable> --root <deliverable checkout> --claim-root <tracking checkout> --brief <tracking brief>`. The claim is taken, released and reported in the tracking repo, where the brief, its Evidence and its outcome record live; it no longer lands in the deliverable repo. `verifyloop plan` prints that exact command for each cross-repository item. The hard-fail for a `--repo` or `--root` that is not the deliverable now names the `--repo` to re-run with. The verifier prompt no longer calls the brief read-only with "implement to its contract": it names the isolated tracking clone as the one place Evidence is edited (#5999).
