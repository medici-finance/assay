Desk-tools provenance must also resolve, from a `desk-tools` or `desk-tools-<os>-<arch>` pin line, a full-commit `desk-tools-source` line, or the in-tree `refs/remotes/origin/main:tools/desk`. A statusgen-only `.assay-versions` that matches main's no longer boots green. The step refuses with exit 6, naming every gap and its remedy. `deskboard` now reads the pin through the same shared reader, so the boot and the board cannot disagree about one file.
