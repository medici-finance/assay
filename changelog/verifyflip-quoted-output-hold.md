### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` and the reconcile fold no longer refuse a strict PASS because a row's recorded output, quoted in ASCII double quotes, contains HELD or could-not-check (the held scan's rule 7). The flip read alone skips such an occurrence, and both callers still require a passing execution witness for every Verify row. The verify-gate card and close-verify read it as before. Quotes pair within a results-table cell, or, on any other line, over both the whole line and its cell, and only when no other line of the paragraph holds a quote. An unpaired or escaped quote, an inch mark, a span holding only the marker word, a possible link-definition title, or a marker outside every quoted span still holds the row.
