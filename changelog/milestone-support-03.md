### Added
- statusgen now loads versioned milestone records for streams that opt in through their README's `reader-requirements` envelope (a required operation naming `milestone-v1`). Records under `docs/streams/<stream>/milestones/` are validated against the milestone contract and the new `schemas/milestone-v1.json`: identity and version come from the frontmatter, never the filename; duplicates, ownership mismatches, unknown keys and wrong-kind references are refused with their locations; missing directories, unreadable or unrecognized entries, uncovered criteria, unmapped work, unverifiable evidence and unloaded prerequisites leave coverage `unknown` instead of reading as empty or complete. A source or evidence path that is absolute or escapes the repository refuses the record. Legacy streams are untouched. The capability stays unadvertised, so until the coordinated rollout no command reads an opted-in stream's records (its coverage stays `unknown`) and the lint and write paths still refuse an opted-in tree.
