### Added
- statusgen now loads versioned milestone records for streams that opt in through their README's `reader-requirements` envelope (a required operation naming `milestone-v1`). Records under `docs/streams/<stream>/milestones/` are validated against the milestone contract and the new `schemas/milestone-v1.json`: identity and version come from the frontmatter, never the filename; duplicates, ownership mismatches, unknown keys and wrong-kind references are refused with their locations; missing directories, unreadable or unrecognized entries, uncovered criteria, unmapped work, unverifiable evidence and unloaded prerequisites leave coverage `unknown` instead of reading as empty or complete. Legacy streams are untouched, and the capability stays unadvertised, so opted-in trees are still refused in production until the coordinated rollout.
