### Fixed
- statusgen's HELD/could-not-check scan (verify-gate card, model autoflip, `--close-verify`) no longer reads the words inside an inline code span, so quoted tool output such as `` `no record is held under the data grant` `` stops suppressing a clean PASS. A span is excluded only where it certainly renders as inline code: an unterminated or mismatched backtick strips nothing, a span never crosses a table-cell pipe, a backtick that may close a span opened earlier in the paragraph leaves the rest of that paragraph read in full, a `<` that may open an autolink or raw HTML leaves the text after it read in full, and a span holding only `HELD` or `could-not-check` still counts (#2100).
