### Fixed
- `scanloop run` no longer writes placeholders that another open scan carrier (PR or MR) already carries. It reads this App's open scan carriers and their changed files through the forge, so a fresh carrier holds only its own delta and the carriers merge in any order. A carrier closed unmerged stops counting, and the next pass writes its placeholders again. If the carriers or their files cannot be read, the pass reports could-not-check and fails closed; it never falls back to the full superset. A pass whose placeholders are all carried already makes no commit and opens no carrier.
