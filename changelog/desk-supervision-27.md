### Added
- Verify-outcome records now carry join keys: `prs` (the merged changes that delivered the brief, each `{repo, number, merge_sha}`, or an explicit empty list with `prs_unknown_reason`), `verifier_tier`, `started`, `finished`, optional `row_results` and optional `dispatch_ref`. `deskevidence --outcome-record` reads each `prs` entry back from the forge (exists, merged, merge commit, in the record's history) and refuses one that fails, and treats an unreadable forge as could-not-check.
- A record landed in a non-private register is redacted by the writer before the outbound scan: a `prs` entry for a repository that is not exactly public becomes a `withheld` digest and the `dispatch_ref` is dropped. On a private register `prs` stays in clear and `dispatch_ref` is replaced by its `dispatch_ref_sha256` digest, written by the writer only.

### Changed
- A `verify-fail` or `blocked` outcome record now needs a complete verify-wake-v1 receipt, and the refusal names the first missing field. A `verified` record that claims verify-wake-v1 lists what delivered the brief; legacy-shape `verified` records need nothing new.
- `deskevidence --outcome-record` refuses malformed input shapes before any forge read: malformed or out-of-range `prs` entries, an unknown `verifier_tier` or one that disagrees with the admitted tier, inverted `started`/`finished`, a `row_results` list that does not match `rows_total`/`rows_passed`, a badly shaped `dispatch_ref`, writer-only keys, and repeated or case-variant keys. A redaction that would move the record's brief, `ts` or admitted path is refused with nothing written.
