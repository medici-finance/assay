### Fixed

- `deskdispatch` reads the human gate from the brief an item key names. With no `--brief`, a brief-shaped key (`<repo>--<stream>--<NN>`, `<stream>/<NN>`, alias-prefixed) resolves to `docs/streams/<stream>/brief-<NN>-*.md` under `--root`, then `--claim-root`. A `gate: human` brief found this way runs the decision gate, takes a recorded ruling, and is held pre-claim on an unruled decision, as it would with `--brief`. A brief-shaped key whose brief matches no file, or more than one, is refused before the claim (exit 5), naming the key and the paths tried. `decision-gate SKIPPED: item is not human-gated` now prints only when the brief was read and does not gate, and the line names that brief, or when the item names no brief (`issue-<N>`). `--gate-human` and an explicit `--brief` behave as before. The derived brief feeds only the human gate: deliverable resolution, the exec-tier check, write scopes, the prompt's specification line and the dispatch record still read `--brief` alone.
