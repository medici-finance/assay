### Added
- desk-containers/25 brief: `cellctl desk --force` diagnoses a failed desk boot and applies the matching repair. Safe repairs run at once. Destructive ones run only on a persisted house cell (read from cell.env itself) with no per-run `--kind`, and only after the run holds the role lease (taken by the boot, or obtained by the stale-holder row on its `Diagnose` proof); they are confirmed first, preserve what they displace in a local `refs/heads/rescue/*` ref built off-branch and an aside tree, and never move the role branch; `-y` confirms them, but it never answers the reconcile preflight. Repairs that `cellctl reset`, the reconcile preflight or `deskwt` already own stay with those verbs, and a live session is never ended. A persisted house cell refuses a per-run non-house `--kind` on `desk` and `up` (with or without `--set`), and a `CELL_KIND` change needs a stopped cell. Authoring only; nothing is implemented.
