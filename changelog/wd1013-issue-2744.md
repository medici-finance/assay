### Fixed
- `deskdispatch` now refuses (exit 5) before any claim or worktree when a `--brief` declares an `exec-tier:` stricter than `--tier`, and the refusal names both values. A dry run refuses too. It also holds a fresh worker dispatch of a `gate: human` brief whose decision issue is open, carries `needs-decision` and has no ruling from the ratifying identity since that label was applied. The hold names the issue, which is found from the brief's `decision-issue:` field or else from its decision-gate marker. A decision that cannot be read, or one whose issue does not exist yet, does not hold (medici-finance/assay#2744).
- `deskboard dispatch` drops the same rows: unruled human decisions always, and briefs stricter than the new `--tier` option when it is given. It counts them in the held-back line (`heldByExecTier`, `heldByDecision`), names rows it could not check, and tags each row with its `execTier`. `deskboard throughput` no longer counts decision-held rows in the dispatch depth (medici-finance/assay#1958).
- Keep all six ruling-history mutations pointed at the shared ruling helper after its move, preserving the existing timestamp, identity, label-removal and incomplete-history assertions.
