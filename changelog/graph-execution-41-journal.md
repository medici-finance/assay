### Added
- The loop-admin journal now holds the rest of its process facts in PostgreSQL (graph-execution/41, design `DR-graph-exec-41`, accepted under option 1). A second forward-only migration adds admitted immutable limits, usage reports, checkpoints, referenced artifacts, operator-set profile budgets and an append-only ledger of consumed launches. Each launch is admitted under one database lock shared by every caller: a missing budget, an unreadable count, an unknown launch of the profile or a full window refuses it, and a pacing estimate may delay a launch but never refuses one on its own. Restore provenance from the operator source holds a restored journal paused until the incarnation rotates. `Inventory` and `CheckRestore` verify the referenced-artifact inventory around an operator's backup and restore and never launch. The migration creates a migration-owner role and a runtime role that cannot rewrite or delete launch rows, write budgets or limits, or change the schema. The test fixture now accepts only a loopback server's admin database and drops only databases it marked. `docs/loop-admin.md` describes the journal for operators.

### Changed
- CI runs the journal's three new PostgreSQL tests in the `loopadmin-journal.yml` job against a disposable server, and the `ci.yml` floor job skips them.
