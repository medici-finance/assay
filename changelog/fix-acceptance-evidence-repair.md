- statusgen: an acceptance-transition descriptor may end with `Evidence-Repair: commit=<sha> approval=<url>` lines. A repair that the driver (the blessing login) approved in an unedited issue or pull-request comment that names the brief and its own thread and carries a plain declarative `I authorize` sentence (no question mark, markup, or negating or conditional word such as `not`, `don't`, `if`, `unless`, `would` in any sentence that mentions authorizing, and no refusal word such as `reject` or `withdraw` anywhere; a scope limit in another sentence is fine), and that only split `Date / Runner` after the leading date or added an `(on-behalf-of human:<name>)` principal, moves the historical-Evidence byte-prefix baseline to the Evidence at the repair commit. The comparison stays byte-strict. A repair that touches a Verify row, changes any other cell, drops earlier text, adds a marker, verdict, comment, fence, HTML or link-reference/footnote definition line, splits a table, splits only part of a table's rows, or changes what the Evidence readers see is refused, and so is a bot-authored, edited, unnamed or other-human approval. Without the line, a reshaped history refuses as before.
- deskevidence: an Evidence-repair approval read that could not be checked (`evidence repair <12 hex>: could-not-check human authority: ...`, in either acceptance-transition form) is a known could-not-check producer, so on a brief the write does not touch it is reported, not refused.
