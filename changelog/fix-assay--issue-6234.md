### Fixed
- `deskflip` checks-green, `deskautolane` merge checks-green and `deskpost ready` now read the check suites at the head, not only the check-run rollup. A suite with no conclusion and zero runs never appears in the rollup but still holds the forge's merge gate, so a green rollup flipped or merged a head the forge reported `BLOCKED`. Such a suite now holds checks-green as could-not-check (exit 6), naming the suite id, as do a failed or short suite read. A later suite, even a green rerun of the same workflow, releases nothing: the suite holds until it concludes (a `cancelled` or `stale` conclusion included) or a new head is pushed. An installed app that never reports at the head is ignored.
- New read-only forge method `CheckSuitesAtHead` (GitHub: `commits/{sha}/check-suites`, under `checks: read`; GitLab: no suite concept, answers an empty set with no request).
