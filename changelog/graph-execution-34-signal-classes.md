### Added
- The workflow-pattern schema can express a signal-instantiated pattern: an input item may name a `signal` (with a source, a liveness check and the classes it admits), a decision node may list `options` and a recommended `default`, a containment decision lists the closed `containment` taxonomy, and a top-level `signal-classes` states each class's exits, admission, culprit record, remedies, refused paths and limits. `statusgen patterns --lint` enforces eight new rules over them, with admission and the `.github/` floor checked fail-closed on the presence of any effect-bearing node; no role gains an effect kind, and no runtime reads a signal pattern yet.
