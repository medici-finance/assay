### Changed
- The verify desk now lands verdicts through the project's verdict-transcription lane where the project has armed it: `verifyloop verdict` composes and signs the verdict, the project's verdict filer files it as an issue, and `statusgen --transcribe-verdict` writes the Evidence and `gate: model` flip on `main`. In an armed project, direct `deskevidence` writes to `main` become break-glass (lane down, the driver's go for that landing). Evidence that is not PASS, outcome records and irreversible-brief Evidence take the PR-form residual route. Projects that have not armed the lane keep `deskevidence` as their standing path, unchanged. The shared git-push-policy block in every desk skill carries the same rule.
