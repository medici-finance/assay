### Fixed
- `fanoutloop plan` now offers open issues addressed `to:worker` in the plan's own repo that no placeholder in any configured root carries, in the leading addressed lane. Each one must pass the trust, decision-label, label-history and dedupe gates first: an issue whose label history shows a decision-owed, `help wanted` or system-state label applied or removed, or whose history is incomplete or unreadable, is held. Every inbox issue it does not offer is listed under `INBOX NOT OFFERED` with the reason, and a failed inbox read prints `INBOX LANE HELD:` instead of reading as an empty inbox.
- `fanoutloop plan --decision-hold <item>=<owner/name>#<N>` re-reads a carried decision hold on every run. A decision issue closed with `human-decided` lifts the hold. Any other read keeps the item out of the queue, and the plan prints the source on the same line. A standing hold that names no item in the queue prints `HOLD UNMATCHED:` and withholds nothing.
