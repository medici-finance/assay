### Added
- `spec/workflow-patterns/signal-triggered-v1.yaml`: a third workflow pattern, instantiated by a signal rather than a brief. It runs contain (a closed quarantine, rollback, secondary-auth or stop taxonomy), confirm and de-duplicate, root-cause with a mandatory replay fixture, and propose (a draft PR, never a merge). Its exits are a human mitigation decision for an outage, the review lane for a regression, and a human decision with a recommended default for a gate-red judgement.
- `statusgen patterns --lint` checks signal patterns: gate-red admission (only `schedule` or `push` to `main`), the closed mechanical remedy set and the gate-configuration path floor, a default on the judgement decision, withheld-detail gates always `judgement`, and a mandatory replay fixture. The schema gains optional signal keys and a closed `effects[].kind` enumeration.
