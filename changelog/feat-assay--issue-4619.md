### Changed
- `statusgen verifyrun` container mode now admits a version-2 (cross-repository) receipt instead of refusing it. Pass `--root` naming the deliverable home and `--brief` naming the tracking brief, which must sit outside that home. The receipt must name two different `owner/name` repositories, and the brief must hold exactly the bytes its `BriefSHA256` records. The attestation line names both repositories. At open and close, the host check's admitted home must be `--root`. At close, the host binding must equal the receipt's field for field. Every version-1 check is unchanged.
