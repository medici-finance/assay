### Added
- `statusgen --lint`: the committer-identity independence check keeps its hard failure for a same-identity author/verifier pair, and now has one recorded way through it for a legitimate post-verification re-touch. A brief may carry `independence-override: "human:<name> YYYY-MM-DD <reason>"` in its frontmatter. It is honored only when the name is a current human who is not the brief's author, the committed line was last written by a git identity other than the one it excuses, and the Evidence section has not changed since that commit. An identity cannot record or edit its own override; an uncommitted, stale or malformed override is refused, and the failure says why. An honored override is reported as a notice, and the `human:<name>` on an added override line is corroborated online like any other human stamp.
