### Added
- New `perfdata` module with a `perfdata record` / `perfdata report` command. It records pseudonymous local performance observations (`performance-event-v1`) into `<state>/performance-events/` and reports on them offline. It has no network, database or service dependency and uses the standard library only. Recording is armed only by `ASSAY_TELEMETRY=1` together with an explicitly selected `tool` or `workflow` profile (`--profile` or `ASSAY_PERFDATA_PROFILE`). `ASSAY_TELEMETRY=1` alone selects no profile and creates nothing. Workflow correlation (work, session, agent and invocation links) is written only as random aliases scoped to a source epoch. The alias map stays local. Argv, paths, titles, bodies, raw errors and credentials are never written. The records are pseudonymous, not anonymous.
- The local store is bounded and tolerates crashes. It is segmented and append-only, and a lock serializes writers. Identity is kept per source, epoch and sequence, and a crash starts a new epoch instead of reusing an identity. Each source has a coverage manifest (missing, empty-complete, complete, partial or corrupt) with partial tails, gaps, drops, unclean terminations and retirements. A policy must name a byte cap. Raw data has a 1–7 UTC-day age cap, and optional daily distribution rollups can be kept for up to 90 days. Every retirement is reported as unsent loss.
- The local report counts nothing twice. Elapsed time is the union of intervals, with summed wall time shown beside it. Only own CPU is summed. A cumulative usage snapshot replaces earlier snapshots of its series. Pooled average and P95 come from merged distributions, and unknown values are counted as unknown, never as zero.
- `spec/performance-event-v1.md` (draft), `schemas/performance-event-v1.json` and `schemas/performance-batch-v1.json` publish the event, batch and receipt contract. The `perfdata/testdata/api/` cases are a conformance suite that a future collector can test against. Human acceptance of the field allowlist, loss envelope and writer overhead is pending.

### Changed
- `docs/telemetry.md`: the anonymized, counts-only promise now explicitly covers `telemetry-v1` only. A new section describes the separate local `performance-event-v1` record class and its consent. The telemetry-v1 endpoint, switches and schema are unchanged.
