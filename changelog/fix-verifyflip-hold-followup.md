### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` and the reconcile fold no longer skip a double-quoted hold inside a verifyrun witness row (the held scan's rule 7). The witness check credits such a row by the first state word in its Result cell, so a Result cell reading `"<hold>" pass exit=0` flipped where it had been refused before rule 7; it is refused again on every read. Whether a row is a witness row is read on the comment-stripped text the witness check reads, so an HTML comment that hides the row's witness shape on its source line, or joins two source lines into one row, does not reopen the skip.
- The held scan's rule 7 no longer reads a span from quote characters a renderer may not show as a pair: a `<` or `&` in the cell or line (quotes inside HTML comments, a `&quot;` reference beside an ASCII quote), a quote beside a non-ASCII character (a no-break space), or a quote with white space on both sides (a spaced ditto or inch mark, a form feed or vertical tab counting as white space), or link or image syntax in the region (a quote in a link or image title or a bare destination). Such a hold is refused on every read.
