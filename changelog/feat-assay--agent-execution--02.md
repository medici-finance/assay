### Added
- `loopadmin/workspace` implements prepared-environment/v1 (`spec/prepared-environment-v1.md`, `schemas/prepared-environment-v1.json`). A receipt records the exact repository revisions, recipe, tools, skills, harness bundle, optional image, writable paths and policy references of one instance workspace, and what an observer reported it to be. It holds no credential material at any depth and is decoded strictly, and refusals name the field, never the value.
- The `workspace.Port` prepares, reconciles and releases instance workspaces over injected resource, Git, authority, store and retirement adapters. A workspace is ready only when every mandatory input matches what the adapters observe, and the port stores a ready receipt only after it survives the same strict decode a caller admits it through. A changed invocation or policy rebinds under current authority. Unknown or partial attempts are reconciled before a retry, and nothing is checked out over unpublished or unknown files. Release never deletes: it holds on a missing receipt, an expired or unknown allocation, or unpublished files, and otherwise delegates to the retirement owner.
- Readiness binds to the instance and its generation. Instances of one cell share a compatible preparation but never a writable workspace. A successor gets its own binding, and a restoration records its source and continuity, refusing an unsupported or unknown need.
- `runner.DecodeStrict` and `runner.CheckPreservation` expose the request's strict decoder and the execution profile's continuity check, so layered contracts can reuse them.
