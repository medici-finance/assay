### Changed
- The desk-tools README's role-credential provider section now has a callsite inventory. The nine write verbs, `repohardenguard` and every caller of the shared resolver read in process. `deskpost` keeps its own in-process reviewer mint, separate from the provider. Only the boot, cold-mint and release checks of the installed binary still run `desktoken` as a child. The inventory also lists the places that forward a credential they already hold, or the path of its file, to a child: `deskdispatch` to the claim tool, the decision-gate script and the `statusgen` recorded-ruling check, `deskevidence` to the `statusgen` host audit, `cellctl` to `deskd` (GitHub) and to `git` (GitLab), `scanloop` to its poller, `deskgit` to `git` on a push, `deskwt` to `git` through a credential helper, `deskreconcile` to `statusgen`, and `statusgen` on to `gh`.
- `docs/library-first.md` no longer says that moving desk verbs off the `desktoken` child is deferred.
- The design record for the role credential provider now records the driver's chosen option, says that the choice covers #4408 only, and stays unratified until a ruling comment names the record.
