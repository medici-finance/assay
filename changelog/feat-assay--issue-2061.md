### Changed
- `pr-review-desk`: a content-scan refusal on a verdict body is now an explicit STOP. The desk never rewords, re-encodes, splits, trims or resends the refused body. It files the refusal at discovery, records on the PR that a verdict is withheld, and keeps dispatching the queue. The scan override is the maintainer's alone. Only the reviewer may re-issue its own verdict, with `path:line` citations in place of quotations. The reviewer kit (`deskdispatch --kit review` §12) carries the reviewer's half, and a new scenario test fails any "reword after scan refusal" transcript.
