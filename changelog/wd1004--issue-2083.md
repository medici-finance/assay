### Fixed
- The review-finding ledger no longer merges findings from different review lanes that reuse an id. The correctness and security reviewers each number their own findings, so on a PR reviewed by both, `A3` named two unrelated findings and the derived ledger folded them into one (one lane's class, evidence and state leaking into the other). Findings, their per-class round counters and their arbiter packets are now keyed by lane and id. A reviewer record speaks only for its own lane (a block naming another lane is reported could-not-check), a worker reply's stated lane wins, and a lane-less worker reply attaches to the one lane holding the id or is reported could-not-check when two lanes hold it. The lane vocabulary is closed (`correctness`, `security`, `fact-check`, `fail-first`, case-folded) and `/` is refused in an id, class or lane. `reviewloop`'s rendered and compact ledgers print the bare id and class with a separate `lane=` field, so a successor agent that copies them reuses the finding instead of forking it. `deskpost`'s content-defect check reads a review's lane through the same classifier as its verdict gate (a body claiming both verdicts resolves neither lane), and a structural test fails if any reader builds a ledger record without a lane.
