### Fixed
- The review-finding ledger no longer merges findings from different review lanes that reuse an id. The correctness and security reviewers each number their own findings, so on a PR reviewed by both, `A3` named two unrelated findings and the derived ledger folded them into one (one lane's class, evidence and state leaking into the other). Findings, and their per-class round counters, are now keyed by lane and id (`security/A3`, `correctness/A3`); a lane-less worker reply attaches to the one lane holding the id and is reported could-not-check when two lanes hold it. `deskpost`'s content-defect check and `reviewloop --records` now carry the lane, and a structural test fails if any reader builds a ledger record without one.
