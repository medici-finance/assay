### Changed
- The hourly board reconcile job writes `statusgen --signoff-digest` to its job summary: every brief awaiting a human sign-off, with its Evidence link, oldest first once the status historian has ages. The step is read-only and posts nothing to issues or comments. Like every board call it passes `--acceptance-forge`, reading through the job's own read-only `github.token`, never an App token. A could-not-check digest prints its could-not-check body and a warning, and the tick goes on. Lands with a maintainer's workflow push.
