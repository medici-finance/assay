### Fixed
- The evidence-automerge `Request auto-merge` step no longer dies before its refusal classifier runs. Under the default `bash -e` shell a refused `enablePullRequestAutoMerge` ended the step at the capture line, so a refusal the classifier calls benign (unstable or clean status) still reddened the run with no log of GitHub's answer; the capture now records the real exit status and the classifier always decides. The fix is in the staged copy and the ci-load activation copy; the live workflow picks it up when a maintainer re-promotes the staged file. A new offline test runs the extracted step under `bash -e` against a failing `gh` stub, with a reverted-fix mutation and a guard that flags any other capture-then-`$?` step in the staged workflows.
