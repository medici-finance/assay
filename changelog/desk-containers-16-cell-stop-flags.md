### Added
- Cell-scoped stop flags: `STOP.cell.<cell>` stops every desk loop of one cell and `STOP.cell.<cell>.<loop>` one loop of it, where a session belongs to a cell only through the `ASSAY_CELL` marker `cellctl` sets. The Guard refuses every outward verb under either flag, and the `pr-review-desk`, `verify-desk` and `worker-desk` iteration-boundary checks and the `cellctl` cadence supervisor read them too. Cell flags only add stops (precedence `DISABLED` > `STOP` > `STOP.cell.<cell>` > `STOP.<loop>` = `STOP.cell.<cell>.<loop>`); a marker outside `[a-z0-9-]` is refused exit 6 before any path is built; `deskboard` lists cell flags, tags one INERT only when its cell name breaks the rule or its loop part names no known loop, and a cell with no registration under the cells root as the board's own shell resolves it is also tagged INERT, with a reason that names the root it looked in and says the flag may still be live (a cell registered under another root is still stopped).

### Changed
- `cellctl` holds every cell name, a container cell and a cell's `CELL=` value included, to one rule: a single directory under the cells root spelled with lower-case letters, digits and `-` (`[a-z0-9-]`), the rule the cell-scoped stop flags name a cell by. A nested name (`team/demo`), an upper-case letter, `_` or `.` is refused by `new`, `set` and every verb that loads a cell; a cell made earlier under such a name no longer loads and must be renamed (its directory, and `CELL=` in its `cell.env`).
