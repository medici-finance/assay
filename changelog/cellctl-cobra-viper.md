### Changed
- `cellctl` now parses its command line with Cobra and resolves its per-run settings through the shared desk adapter. Every verb, flag and precedence rule is preserved, and every refusal is preserved except where the recorded decision `DR-cellctl-cobra` names the change: a line of a shape the old parser refused (a flag or a lone `-` before the verb or ahead of a verb's positionals, `--cells-root` anywhere but first or in another spelling, the single-dash spelling of a long flag outside `scratch`) is refused again, exit 3, before anything runs, and a relative `--cells-root` is refused first on every line, as before. `cadence`, `cache` and `comms` still compare `--confirm-stopped` as an exact word in its place, `check` and `deskd` still read the words after their cell by position, and a repeated `--kind` keeps its first value. `--help`, `<verb> --help` and `--version` read no cell or credential (and the roster only to refuse a relative `--cells-root`), `--flag=value` and `--` are accepted, and a parse failure carries Cobra's wording with the usage exit code (3, including `scratch`, which used 2, and `desk`, which used 1 for a flag-shaped word in its cell's place). The differences are listed in `docs/cellctl-cli-compat.md` against `DR-cellctl-cobra`, and the generated command list is `docs/cellctl-cli-reference.md`. The hidden shell-completion entrypoints are refused like any unknown command.
