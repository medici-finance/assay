### Changed
- `cellctl` now parses its command line with Cobra and resolves its per-run settings through the shared desk adapter. Every verb, flag, precedence rule and refusal is preserved: a line of a shape the old parser refused (a flag before the verb or ahead of a verb's positionals, `--cells-root` anywhere but first or in another spelling, the single-dash spelling of a long flag outside `scratch`) is refused again, exit 3, before anything runs. `--help`, `<verb> --help` and `--version` now read no cell, credential or roster, `--flag=value` and `--` are accepted, and a parse failure carries Cobra's wording with the usage exit code (3, including `scratch`, which used 2). The differences, including the ones still awaiting a ruling, are listed in `docs/cellctl-cli-compat.md`, and the generated command list is `docs/cellctl-cli-reference.md`. The hidden shell-completion entrypoints are refused like any unknown command.
