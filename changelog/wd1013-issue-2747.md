### Fixed
- A dispatch that was prepared but never launched no longer sits silently behind its claim. `deskdispatch` now ends with a `NOT LAUNCHED` line (also under `--quiet`) naming the claim, the grace window (`CLAIMED_TTL_MIN`, default 20 minutes) and the two ways forward: launch the agent and run the claim tool's `progress`, or `release` it. `deskclaim-ref show` and `list` mark a claim that never progressed past that window as `PREPARED-NOT-LAUNCHED`, and the next dispatcher reclaims it.
