### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` now cross-checks the brief's latest verify-outcome record before flipping a row to verified. The flip refuses (exit 1) when no record names the brief, when the record names another revision than `--sha` (its `source_sha`, or `sha` on older records without one), or when its outcome is not `verified`, and the refusal says which of the three it was. An unreadable record store, a malformed record, a record with no revision, or a record dated beyond the clock-skew tolerance is could-not-check (exit 2). Before this, a strict PASS in Evidence could be flipped while the run's own record said `blocked`. A brief flipped by hand with no matching record now refuses until its verify run writes one.
