### Fixed
- `statusgen verifyflip` now cross-checks the brief's latest verify-outcome record before flipping a row to verified. The flip refuses (exit 1) when no record names the brief, when the record names another revision than `--sha`, when its outcome is not `verified`, when a record for the brief is uncommitted, or when the deciding record was not added by the bound verifier or a roster human, and the refusal says which it was. The revision is the record's `source_sha`; an older record without one is read by its `sha`, which is usually the commit its Evidence landed at rather than the run's revision, so a mismatch there asks for a re-run. An unreadable record store, a malformed record, a record with no revision, a record whose `ts` is not RFC 3339, a record not at the path its own bytes name, a legacy log line naming the brief that the legacy reader skipped, a deciding legacy log line, or a record dated beyond the clock-skew tolerance is could-not-check (exit 2). Before this, a strict PASS in Evidence could be flipped while the run's own record said `blocked`. A brief flipped by hand with no matching record now refuses until its verify run writes one.
