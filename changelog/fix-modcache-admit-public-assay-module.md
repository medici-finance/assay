### Fixed
- The host module-cache bracket can admit modules from the public Assay repository (`github.com/medici-finance/assay`) only after a fresh unauthenticated identity and visibility check, with exact committed module versions and both checksums recorded in the receipt and revalidated against the admitted source. Private or unknown repository metadata and operator-private patterns refuse before any download or receipt. The cluster stager retains its owner-wide denial; its hermetic tests catch a planted unproven-prefix bypass. No owner-wide public allowance is introduced.

### Changed
- The proof applies to every Assay module path whatever the verified owner is, so it is stricter for adopters outside the Assay owner. Their Assay requirements previously passed the screen as ordinary `github.com` paths with no extra request. They now need one unauthenticated GET to `api.github.com`, which counts against GitHub's anonymous rate limit, and the open step refuses with nothing downloaded on a 403 (the rate limit included), any other failed response or a network failure, and when `go.sum` carries only the `/go.mod` checksum for a requested Assay version. A require that a versioned `replace` points at another module also refuses until `go.sum` carries the Assay version's own checksums.
- The open step screens a copy of each root's `go.mod` and `go.sum` taken before the screen, and refuses a root whose files changed between the screen and its download.
