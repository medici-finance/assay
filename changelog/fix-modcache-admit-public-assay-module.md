### Fixed
- The module screen that runs before `statusgen verifyrun --open-bracket --module-cache` downloads anything, and its twin in the verify-runner image's `stage-cache.sh`, now admit the public Assay modules (`github.com/medici-finance/assay/...`, such as `tools/desk`) even when the verified repository shares their owner. Before, a consumer tree that requires `tools/desk` was refused because every path under its owner was treated as private. Every other path under that owner, including look-alike neighbours (`assay-other`, `assay2`, `assayx`) and a path in another case, is still refused, and the operator's private patterns still refuse the public modules too. A parity test holds the two lists equal.
