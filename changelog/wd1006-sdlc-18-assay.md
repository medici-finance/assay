### Added
- Decision-gate hold (`spec/lifecycle-v1.md` §4.5): a `gate: human` brief can no longer move to `implemented`, `verified` or `done`, or have its gate relabelled or the brief dropped, until a human has ruled on its decision issue. A brief records the ruling as a new optional `ruling:` link next to `decision-issue:`. Any ruling lifts the hold, including one that declines the brief.
- `statusgen --lint` gains the hold's offline layer: such a change without a well-formed `ruling:` link is a PROBLEM. The finding says it checked only for a well-formed link, not who wrote the comment. It fails closed when the base revision cannot be resolved or read while a `gate: human` brief sits at `implemented` or later. A brief matched to a base brief by its permanent `id:` alone is in scope but cannot borrow that brief's ruling or its landed status: a new board row that takes the `id:` of a brief still on the board is a move from not on the board. Identity is one-to-one: a brief removed while its `id:` is parked on a brief already on the board is a drop; a renumber pairs one base brief with one new board id, so one ruling cannot serve two renumbered rows and one row cannot absorb two base briefs. A board id that resolves to more than one record (two README rows or brief files under spellings such as `018` and `18`, or records under both `docs/streams` and `docs/archive`) is judged from its lowest status and any `gate: human`, and a change that leaves such a collision in scope is refused until each brief has its own board id; an untouched collision stays quiet. Both sides of a change are read by one rule: a stream index, brief file, `done/` folder, stream directory or board tree that is a symbolic link, a submodule or a nested repository, or a board file that cannot be read, is an unreadable record — refused when a change leaves it, and judged as `gate: human` with no ruling at the base, never as carrying no gate. The README table is the board: a `gate: human` brief whose README row is removed is dropped even when its brief file stays at the stream root, under `done/` or in the archive tree.
- `statusgen --decision-gate --pr <N>` (and `--decision-gate-base <rev>`) is the hold's network layer. It fetches the linked comment and requires an unedited comment by a human login on the brief's own decision issue that names the brief. `--corroborate --pr` and `--close-verify` run the same check. Exit 0 clean, 1 refused, 2 usage.
- `deskevidence` refuses a landing that would introduce a decision-gate refusal, and reports could-not-check (exit 6) when `statusgen` is missing or predates `--decision-gate`.
- `deskpr create` prepends an informational banner to a PR that delivers a `gate: human` brief, naming its decision issue and whether a ruling is linked. The banner blocks nothing.
- `statusgen init` scaffolds a `decision-gate` pull-request job. Making it a required check is a repository-admin act; `docs/adopting-assay.md` says so. The hold never runs at a PR's ready-flip.
- This repository's own workflow changes for the hold (a `--decision-gate` step in `assay-statusgen.yml`, a refusal branch in `verify-gate-close.yml`) are staged under `ci/staged-workflows/` pending maintainer promotion.
