### Changed
- A standing hold a desk carries forward (a do-not-flip or do-not-dispatch rule) now names its source on the same line in the standing note and the handoff record. At its next fresh sweep the desk re-reads each source. It drops and reports a hold only when the hold's own lift condition is met or the identity that set it releases it; a closed issue or merged PR is not a lift. A hold whose source cannot be read is kept as could-not-check, and a hold whose source cannot be found is kept, reported as unsourced and escalated to the driver once. Dropping a hold line never lifts a gate enforced elsewhere. A summary across context compaction can no longer quietly widen a hold's scope.
