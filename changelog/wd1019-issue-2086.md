### Fixed
- `statusgen --autonomy`: the deterministic-gate share no longer runs out of time on a busy window. It reads the merged PRs' status-check rollups in GraphQL requests of 20 PRs each, not one `gh pr view` per PR, so a window under the 500-row listing cap is measured inside the 2-minute deadline. A head commit with more than 100 checks is read page by page until a gate check appears or the checks end, so it is never counted from its first 100 checks alone. A window at the cap still reports `listing-cap`; narrow it with `--since`.
