### Fixed
- Forge response bodies the desk tools read are now capped at 32 MiB (`deskkit.MaxForgeBodyBytes`). The cap covers the forge library (including `deskkit.ReadForgeBody`), the credential mint and rotation reads, `deskpost`, `deskrelease`, `desktoken coverage`, `deskadvisory` and the `deskinbox` comments reader. A body over the cap is refused as could-not-check (exit 6) and never read as a truncated answer.
- The cap also applies inside the GitHub and GitLab forge clients' transport. It covers the bodies those client libraries read themselves: error bodies read whole to build an error, answers decoded straight from the stream, and GraphQL batches. A body past the cap fails the request and is never handed back short. The run-log readers keep their own, larger bounds.
- The `deskinbox` comments walk and the `desktoken coverage` repositories walk stop after 100 pages and report could-not-check (or an error) instead of following a forge that never stops answering full pages.
- `deskgitlab` no longer discards a response body read error either. An answer whose body is cut off, stalls, exceeds the 4 MiB bound, or is empty where a GET's result belongs is treated as no answer. A run that meets one exits 6 (could-not-check).
- The `deskevidence` decision-gate check (`statusgen --decision-gate`) is now killed if it runs longer than half the write-window bound (30s each; a landing runs it twice, so 60s in all), and the landing reports could-not-check (exit 6). Before, the landing waited on it with no limit while holding the preparation lock.
