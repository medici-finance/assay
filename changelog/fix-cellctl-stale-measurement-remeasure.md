### Fixed
- `cellctl desk` and scratch launches no longer refuse on a stale, unstamped, future-dated or missing go-cache measurement alone: the launch re-measures once with the same clean `cellctl cache <cell> clean` runs, then admits or refuses on the fresh numbers. It never cleans for size or free-space pressure on a fresh measurement. The one re-measure is the full clean, so when its fresh walk finds the cache past its high-water mark or under pressure it trims, or removes the roots whole when no consumer is live, before the launch decides. It never re-measures twice and never loops. `cellctl up` now also takes its one clean on an unstamped, future-dated or missing measurement.
