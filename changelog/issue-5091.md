### Fixed
- The release workflow tests again check that every mutation harness and every `deskmerge` shard in the release test matrix runs exactly once, uploads its report and keeps its verdict gate. The test matrix must hold exactly its eight legs, including the suites that are not mutation harnesses. A harness step, the report upload or the test job that tolerates failure, a surviving mutation that exits successfully, a shell override (including a workflow-level `defaults` placed after the jobs), an excluded leg, a disabled or non-failing report upload, a publishing job that stops waiting on the test job, and any job-level `if` on a publishing job are each refused. Settings are read by a closed reader (`tools/release/workflow_keys.py`) that accepts only the forms the two release workflow copies and `ci.yml` use, each read exactly as written: a trailing comment is ignored, a setting kept only in a comment counts as absent, keys are read without case, quoted values are accepted only when they hold no escape sequence or doubled quote, a `uses` value must be an action pinned to a full commit SHA, and a bare `|` block scalar is accepted only as a step's `run` or a step's `with` `path`. Every other line, wherever it sits in the file (a quoted key, a space before the colon, a flow mapping, an anchor or merge key, a repeated key, an escaped or multi-line value, a `>` or chomped block scalar, a tab or another control or separator character), is refused rather than skipped, and each job's and step's text is cut where that reader ends it; a step name used twice in one job counts as no step at all. The `artifacts`, `runner-arm64` and `release` jobs and their steps refuse `continue-on-error` in any form; the two optional release Go cache steps are excepted only while each step's whole content matches its pin and its name is used once, so another step cannot borrow the exemption by taking a cache step's name. The workflow environment must hold only `GO_VERSION`, and the test job must not set its own. The publishing jobs refuse step `defaults` (at the workflow or job level), a `shell` or `working-directory` on any step, any job environment but their pinned one, and a step `if` other than the step's pinned condition. They also check that the `artifacts` and `release` jobs bind their fixed runner labels with no expression, that the publisher's runner label matches exactly (a label that merely contains it, or appears only in a comment, is refused), and that the native arm64 job downloads every build artifact (every download step it has) and fails on an empty upload. The publisher's approval, step order, dry-run guards and native arm64 directory, every job's permissions, `persist-credentials: false` on every checkout in the test and publishing jobs, and the upload and download settings (the platform uploads, the native image's upload and downloads, the report upload and the publisher's build download, each held to exactly its pinned inputs; the platform upload paths hold no `!` exclusion line and the native upload path holds exactly the archive and its receipt; no other step in any job has an upload name that is, or through an expression can become, a `release-build-` name) are read from the reader's keys, so a line in a comment, a run script or a `with` block scalar never counts as a step, key or value. Only `release` may grant a write, the workflow default must be `contents: read` alone, a job may not name its environment with an expression, and the test and publishing jobs may not run in a `container`. The publisher's script checks, including the restore step's two `sha256sum -c` checks, look for fixed lines among the live, non-comment lines of one named step's `run` script, so the same text in another key or step does not count; they do not prove the shell executes the line, and a step's own `env` in a publishing job is not read. The test job's suite steps that are not mutation harnesses are not read beyond the matrix that schedules them. A new `tools/release/ci_step_test.py` checks from outside the image contract suite that CI still runs it on the `rest` shard, fails the shard when it fails (no shell override, no excluded shard) and stops it within 15 minutes, and that the Go regression test that also runs every release suite is unchanged; the CI step check reads `ci.yml` through the same strict reader, refuses a job-level `if` or `env` on the shard job, holds the step to its `name`, `if`, `timeout-minutes` and `run` keys, and pins the Go regression test file whole, so the test cannot be commented out or moved into a string.
