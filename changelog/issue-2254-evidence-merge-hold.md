### Fixed
- `deskevidence`'s draft lane (the Evidence row or outcome record it opens as a draft change when the default branch takes no direct write, as on GitLab) now opens the desk's merge-hold marker thread on that change, the same way `deskpr create` does. Verifier Evidence drafts no longer get stuck: `deskflip` used to refuse them with "carries no merge-hold marker thread" even after the reviewer approved. Every desk verb now opens its change through a single helper that also opens the hold, and a structural test fails on any change-opening call that skips it. Evidence drafts opened before this fix still need a hold added by hand or a re-created change.
