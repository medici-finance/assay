### Fixed
- `deskevidence` no longer refuses an Evidence landing on GitLab when the landing checkout's HEAD is the target branch's tip. The decision-gate hold's branch-board read now skips the forge compare when the two are the same commit, tested as exact equality of two full lowercase object ids (never a prefix): the board judged is then the board the write lands on, which is what an `identical` compare with no changed files reported before. When the commits differ the hold is unchanged, so a forge that serves no compare (GitLab) still stops the landing as could-not-check (exit 6). The cross-repository delivery-PR join skips its compare on the same exact test when a delivery PR's merge commit is the admitted source commit.
