### Fixed

- `statusgen --consumers --brief <stream>/<NN>` now passes on merged main when its claims are corroborated, from a checkout detached at `origin/main` with no `--delivery`/`--base`. When the committed Evidence carries no `Verification-Table:` marker, the delivery is the one first-parent commit whose message, or a commit it merged, carries `Brief: <stream>/<NN>`, pinned to its first parent. The search is local only. Zero or several candidates, a shallow history, or a candidate not on the fetched mainline ref is exit 2 (COULD-NOT-CHECK). Explicit flags and the marker still take precedence. Commits and messages are read in process through the Git library, and ancestry uses the existing merge-base reads; a repository the library cannot read is also COULD-NOT-CHECK.
