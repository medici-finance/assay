### Added
- `cellctl` binds a project role secret into worker and verifier containers on a native container cell as a read-only file at `/run/secrets/role-<service>`, named by `ASSAY_ROLE_SECRET_<SERVICE>_FILE` and never passed as a value. `cellctl check` and every launch verify the file's mode, owner, access-control lists and location, and refuse every host-surface binding, the desktop cell included. Each launch writes a receipt that carries no value.
- `deskdispatch` refuses, before claiming, any item it cannot show to be trusted from forge data when dispatching from a window that holds a role-secret path. That covers an untrusted or unblessed issue or change, and a change whose head commits are not linked to roster logins on the base repository. It also strips every role-secret variable from the child processes it starts.
