### Fixed
- The remaining desk-tool refusals and log lines that quoted an origin or remote URL verbatim (deskflip, deskboot and deskdispatch origin resolution, deskdispatch's root-origin check, deskmerge's origin currency and push-destination checks, deskpushguard, deskreconcile, the forge-host resolver, and the push-transport gate's NOTICE, SSH refusals and set-url remedy) now mask its userinfo through `deskkit.RedactRemoteUserinfo`, so a credential-bearing origin cannot leak into a transcript. A failed git or forge command no longer echoes a remote URL from its argv or stderr either: the exec seams of deskpr, deskmerge, deskclose, deskdigest, deskreply, deskwt, deskgit, deskreconcile and scanloop, and the verifier-source reader, redact both. The redactor now masks a password holding an unencoded `/` whole; credentials outside the userinfo (a query string, a path with no `@`) remain out of its scope.
