### Fixed
- The remaining desk-tool refusals and log lines that quoted an origin or remote URL verbatim (deskflip, deskboot and deskdispatch origin resolution, deskdispatch's root-origin check, deskmerge's origin currency and push-destination checks, deskpushguard, deskreconcile, the forge-host resolver, and the push-transport gate's NOTICE, SSH refusals and set-url remedy) now mask its userinfo through `deskkit.RedactRemoteUserinfo`, so a credential-bearing origin cannot leak into a transcript. A failed git or forge command no longer echoes a remote URL from its argv or stderr either: the exec seams of deskpr, deskmerge, deskclose, deskdigest, deskreply, deskwt, deskgit, deskreconcile and scanloop, and the verifier-source reader, redact both. The redactor now masks a password holding an unencoded `/`, quote, bracket, backtick or `://` whole, and masks a userinfo that is a credential alone (`https://<credential>@host`) for http(s) and `ssh://` alike; credentials outside the userinfo (a query string, a path with no `@`) remain out of its scope.
- deskpr's push-destination and multi-fetch-URL refusals now redact the config KEY of a `url.<base>.insteadOf` rule (whose base can carry a userinfo), the unsplit fallback line and the failed-read text, not just the value.
- The credential-chain detail and the failed ambient-transport preflight check no longer keep the user half of a push URL's userinfo, so a credential-only userinfo is masked.
- deskgit now masks userinfo in a failed git call's stderr, and deskpr, deskreply and deskgit strip control sequences from that stderr before redacting it.
- The per-package URL redactor copies in deskpr, deskwt, deskgit and the credential chain are gone: every desk verb renders a remote through `deskkit.RedactRemoteUserinfo` (or its `RedactRemoteURL` / `RedactRemoteConfigKey` renderings), and a guard test fails on a second redactor definition. deskwt's transport refusals keep showing the bare SSH account name `git`.
