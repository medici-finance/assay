### Added
- desk-containers/15: the cell-operation contract `cell-op-v1` (`spec/cell-op-v1.md`, `schemas/cell-op-v1.json`): one result document with fixed outcomes, reasons and exit codes, and one plan document with a JCS digest, a fixed 10-minute expiry and an 8-character confirm code. `tools/desk/internal/cellop` implements the plan store (0600 plans, strict decode), the atomic consume, and the interactive apply order: terminal and desk-session refusals, typed confirm, re-measure, consume, then run, with batch plans consumed before their first child and stopped at a consumed child. `cellctl` gains the adapter mechanics (`cellop:state-changing` annotations, a root gate that refuses annotated invocations off a terminal or in a desk session, `--plan`/`--json`/`--apply` helpers) and a fixture verb built only under the `cellopfixture` tag; no release verb adopts the contract yet. The confirm is a mistake and race guard, not an authorization control; approvals are deferred. How-to: `docs/cellctl-machine-seam.md`.
