### Fixed
- `deskevidence` now lands a cross-repository **verified** outcome record. Its landed-closure check used the read root's `origin/main` as the baseline, but in the private tracking clone that ref is not the tracking repository's main, and the landed commits are not there. The landing refused with `landed commit has no readable main baseline`. The check now fetches the tracking repository's own `main` (and the landing branch) into private `refs/deskevidence-fetch/*` refs and uses that as the baseline. Same-repository landings are unchanged. `gitcore.CheckoutLocalCommit` takes the baseline ref explicitly, and reports an absent landed commit as a missing fetch rather than a history mismatch.
