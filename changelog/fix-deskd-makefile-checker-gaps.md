### Fixed
- The deskd build contract test now reads the root Makefile closer to how GNU Make does, so it can no longer be silently bypassed by any of these: a setting split across backslash-continued lines, `GNUMAKEFLAGS` carrying `-i`, an `exit 256` (which the shell reports as success) before the image build, or a second `deskd-image`/`deskd-build` rule written with spaces, alongside other targets, with its recipe after `;`, as a grouped target (`&:`), through a variable, or with a blank or comment line before its recipe. Any `.RECIPEPREFIX` setting is now refused, and `.PHONY` is read from joined lines with names after a `#` treated as a comment. Each spelling has its own negative fixture.
