### Added
- statusgen: a terminal `withdrawn` brief status, carried by the new brief schema `brief-v3` (brief-v2 plus `withdrawn-date`, `withdrawn-reason` and `superseded-by`; contract `schemas/brief-v3.json`). A withdrawn brief is excluded from the done-ratio denominator, Next-up and dispatch, the stalled/bottleneck/blocked views and the todo count, and is shown in its own Withdrawn count and STATUS.md section. Lint requires brief-v3 and the date/reason keys on a withdrawn brief, rejects the keys anywhere else, resolves same-repo successors, syntax-checks cross-repo successor ids, and raises a NOTICE on any brief whose `depends` names a withdrawn brief.
- statusgen: `statusgen --version --json` prints the brief schemas this build reads and the first release of each; `spec/brief-v1.md` §3.5 carries the schema-version table. A brief whose schema is newer than the binary is refused with the schema it needs, the supported set and the statusgen the repo pins.
- The three withdrawn keys are parsed only on brief-v3 files. On brief-v1 and brief-v2 they stay tolerated unknown keys, so existing boards that use `superseded-by:` as prose lint unchanged.
