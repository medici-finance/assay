### Fixed
- The desk tools that hold the shared audit lock across forge calls (`deskevidence`, `deskpost`, `deskrelease`) and the credential-mint paths they reach (the in-process minter, `desktoken`) now give up on any one forge request after 30 seconds (`deskkit.ForgeRequestTimeout`), so a stalled forge can no longer hold the audit lock indefinitely. This covers the GitHub REST client and its diff read, the GitLab client, and the forge library's former `http.DefaultClient` fallbacks (the repo-visibility and account-liveness readers, the observed-auth client). A client a caller injects with its own non-zero timeout keeps it. Out of scope, and unchanged: tools that never take the audit lock (`deskinbox`, `deskadvisory`, and the label client behind `cellctl`/`deskfleet`/`deskgitlab`, which still fall back to `http.DefaultClient`) and readers that already set their own bound (the composition-source fetch at 60s, the hotfix forge at 30s). A source guard fails the build if a deadline-free client (including through an aliased `net/http` import) appears in the forge library, the credential package or a lock-holding tool.
- In `deskpost` and `deskrelease`, a forge response whose body stalls past that bound, or a success response with an empty body where a result was expected (204/205 excepted), is now a could-not-check (exit 6) instead of being decoded as a complete answer. A truncated check-run or review page can no longer read as an empty, all-clear list. The same source guard refuses a discarded `io.ReadAll` error.
