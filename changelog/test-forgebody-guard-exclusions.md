### Changed
- The forge-body source guard now follows a same-package helper that returns a truncating reader (through helpers that return another helper's call), a truncating alias of any number of hops, and a field path such as `resp.Body` reassigned a truncating reader. It scans function literals outside every function declaration, and it names a method by its receiver type, so two same-named methods are two separate entries.
- In `tools/pairedversions`, whose HTTP client has no transport-level body cap, the guard also reports a response body handed to any call other than its capped reader.
- The guard's header no longer claims every uncapped body read fails the build. It is a syntactic scan with known blind spots. Trees that read forge bodies but sit outside its roots are listed in the guard, and a test fails once one of them no longer shows a hit, so it moves into the roots.
