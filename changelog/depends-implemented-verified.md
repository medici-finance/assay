### Changed
- statusgen: a `gate: human` dependency at `implemented` whose last recorded verdict is a strict `**VERIFY: PASS**` (no later FAIL) now satisfies `depends:` edges, so its dependents become eligible while it waits for human sign-off. `gates:` and `feathers:` edges still wait for `verified`/`done`; a FAIL, a missing verdict, a strict PASS that is only struck, fenced or quoted, a non-human gate, or any other status stays unsatisfied. An unrouted HELD or could-not-check line in the target's Evidence does not hold the `depends:` edge; the human sign-off still refuses on it. The roadmap stream pages, the stalled-critical signal and the flow eligible-at replay still read `depends:` as `done`/`verified` only. The worker-desk skill's dispatch-exclusion line states the same rule.
