### Added
- `deskevidence` stamps the on-behalf-of principal into the Runner cell of every App-run `## Evidence` row a landing adds without one, from the same roster resolution as its commit trailer (the neutral name on a public target, the login on a target the roster states private) and never from argv or the landed content. The annotation goes before a trailing runner-source tag, as `statusgen verifyrun` renders it; rows already on the branch, human-run rows and cells that already carry an annotation are left byte-identical; an unresolvable principal refuses the landing (exit 5) with nothing written.
