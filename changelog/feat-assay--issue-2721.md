### Changed
- `statusgen verifyrun --fail-first` takes `--delivering-commit <rev>` for a post-merge verify: the commit must be HEAD or an ancestor of HEAD and on the default branch's history (the merge-base or an ancestor of it), the base run is its first parent, and the Base cell records `· delivering=<sha>`. Without a delivering commit, the base must be the merge-base itself; an older ancestor is no longer accepted, and a base that is HEAD (the merge-base after a merge) is refused. The `fail-first-closure` lint applies the same rule, and a brief added by its own delivering change stays closable.
