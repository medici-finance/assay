### Fixed
- `deskpost` (and `deskevidence` / `deskrelease`) no longer count a refused audit-lock wait toward the per-PR consecutive no-op circuit breaker. When back-to-back writes held the audit lock for more than the bounded wait, a reviewer retrying as instructed collected one refusal per attempt, tripped the breaker at five, and was then locked out for the 15 minute cooldown even though nothing had been read or sent. The lock-wait refusal (exit 6, raised before any forge read or write) is now audited as a new `contended` result that neither the budget nor the breaker counts. Every other refusal still counts, so a genuine refusal loop still trips at five.
