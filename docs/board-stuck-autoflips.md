# The board's stuck auto-flip roll-up

`STATUS.md` is generated by `statusgen`. This page covers one of its tables,
**Stuck auto-flips**: what puts a row there, what the row means and how it
clears.

## What it shows

A `gate: model` brief reaches `verified` when its Verify rows pass. The model
auto-flip, `statusgen --auto-flip-model`, then moves it to `done`, on two
conditions:

- the brief's coverage is `released`;
- the reviewer App APPROVED the **delivering PR** at that PR's merged head.

When neither of those happens, the brief stays at `verified`, and the roll-up
lists it:

```
### Stuck auto-flips (N)

| Brief | Title | Waiting | Flip result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [s/07](docs/streams/s/README.md) | … | 3d | REFUSED or COULD-NOT-CHECK — `statusgen --auto-flip-model --dry-run` names which and why |
```

The table covers every gate:model brief at `verified`, across every stream,
parked streams included (the flip judges those too). It disappears when there
are none. `Waiting` is how long the brief has been at `verified`, according to
the status history log. It shows `—` when the log has no record of the
transition.

The table is rendered offline from the board files, so it does not say which of
the two results the flip reached. The flip's own output says that, one line per
brief with its reason:

```
statusgen --root . --auto-flip-model --dry-run
```

## The two results

- **REFUSED.** The flip read everything it needed, and a condition fails. For
  example, the delivering PR has no reviewer-App approval at its merged head, or
  the coverage is not `released`. To clear it, fix that condition: get the
  review, or release the coverage.
- **COULD-NOT-CHECK.** The flip could not decide. A forge read may have failed,
  or no delivering PR could be found. One reason worth knowing is
  `no PR touches the brief's files`: the brief declares `files:`, and no merged
  PR crediting it changed any of them. To clear it, correct the `files:` line,
  or land the delivery the brief describes.

## How the delivering PR is chosen

- **By diff shape, not by author.** A PR whose diff only changes verification
  records is never the delivering PR, and no approval is asked of it. That
  covers:
  - lines inside a brief's `## Evidence` section;
  - a stream README's Status, Verified or Reviewed cells, rewritten in place;
  - a verify-outcome record;
  - `STATUS.md`.

  If the forge did not report the PR's patch, or the head content could not be
  read, the PR stays an ordinary candidate, and its own approval is required.
- **By `files:` overlap.** When the brief declares `files:`, the delivering PR
  must touch one of those paths. A PR that credits the brief but touches none of
  them is not chosen, though it still needs its own approval. Entries that name
  another repository (`../<other>/…`) cannot match here.

  A brief without `files:` uses the older rule: the newest approved PR carrying
  its `Brief:` trailer, cross-checked against the brief file's history.

## In CI

`statusgen --auto-flip-model --check` exits **2** when any candidate is REFUSED
or COULD-NOT-CHECK, so a workflow step that runs it fails visibly instead of
hiding a stuck row behind a green run. Other exit codes:

- **1**: a misconfiguration, such as a missing reviewer identity. Exit 1 wins
  over exit 2.
- **0**: without `--check`, a stuck candidate prints a notice and exits 0, the
  earlier behaviour.

`--check` does not imply `--dry-run`. Add `--dry-run` to report without writing.
