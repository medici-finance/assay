# Recover a handoff where it stopped

A fast repair can merge before intake records its judgment. In [#6507](https://github.com/medici-finance/assay/issues/6507) a closed source issue then had no usable handoff to qualification. At the other end of the process, [#6468](https://github.com/medici-finance/assay/issues/6468) documents a tool upgrade between landing Evidence and recording its outcome. The verification branch contained one half of the result, and four other runs stopped after their rows had run. Both cases need a trustworthy next action after a partial success.

These milestones extend the existing intake and verification owners. They measure recoverable cases at their actual handoffs and preserve the authority of independent verification.

| Proposed milestone | Existing work and dependency | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 1. A completed repair leaves intake for qualification | Start at `scanloop land`. At public revision `185b7ee7e`, `checkHandoffSuccessor` returned “successor is not open” even when the source was fixed before intake acted. `deskcore/issueflow` already gives a qualification judgment its own route. | Replay a closed source that a merged change resolves, and a manually closed source whose merged change has its exact issue trailer. An independently checked delivery records one qualification judgment across two attempts, with zero implementation dispatches. Refuse a wrong issue, unrelated change, missing corroboration and unreadable history. Existing `intake-class-routing/02` owns publication to the board; check the committed companion and qualification owner once its publisher is available. |
| 2. A partial verification landing has a specific recovery action | `desk-tools/47` owns interrupted landing, `desk-tools/50` owns environment binding, and `verification-handoff/02–05` own repair disposition and independent recheck. Use `tool-refactoring/08` and /10 for publication, preserving their conditional checks. | Interrupt the real landing path after each write, retry twice and compare the resulting Evidence and outcome IDs. Count duplicate writes, orphaned accepted receipts and unnecessary re-executions. Require one valid result or one explicit recovery obligation per run. A changed tool, source, custody or admission supplies a fresh check wherever required. Never give new credit to incompatible old evidence. |
| 3. A restarted Kubernetes-hosted desk recovers the same work | After the producer and reader versions in milestone 2 qualify together, use `verification-handoff/06` and `tool-refactoring/11` for the joined rollout. An operator supplies the release and cell pin. | With a Kubernetes-hosted desk first, capture one closed-before-intake repair and one interrupted verification. Restart the desk, then inspect intake, claim, Evidence and outcome records at their original identities. Count duplicate worker claims, lost handoffs and unowned waits before and after the restart. Require zero for these two cases and a named next actor for each incomplete result. Repeat in a second supported profile before reporting portability. |

Intake records a judgment about the linked repair. The qualification owner still has to test it. The verifier records which interrupted attempts still qualify. Neither a merged PR nor an old receipt establishes deployment. Source fixtures demonstrate the recovery logic, while plugin adoption, role credentials and a real hosted restart each require independent evidence.
