**Ruling recorded (2026-10-25): option 1.** The brief's human decision block
(`docs/streams/iso-9001/brief-08-source-applicability.md`, `## Human decision`) asked whether
the source-applicability contract is the approved design and whether it becomes a control for
adopting projects. The driver answered on the brief's decision issue,
[issue 4400](https://github.com/medici-finance/assay/issues/4400), in the driver's own login:
[ruling comment](https://github.com/medici-finance/assay/issues/4400#issuecomment-6399479447) —
"approve the source-applicability contract as the design; keep it a library, not activated."

This record transcribes that ruling into the register for the lifecycle's design-approval gate
(`spec/lifecycle-v1.md` §4.4); it does not mint a new decision. It carries no `ruling:` link:
the ruling comment names the brief by its board id but not this record's id, so it would not
satisfy the record-naming condition of the ruling-link check (`spec/registers-v1.md` §7.5,
condition 5). The `decided-by` stamp is corroborated through the other anchors that check
accepts, and the brief's own frontmatter `ruling:` carries the link for the decision-gate hold
(`spec/lifecycle-v1.md` §4.5).

**The decision.** The contract in `spec/project-obligations-v1.md` (schema
`schemas/project-obligations-v1.json`, loader `statusgen/projectobligations.go`) is the approved
design. It records which revision of a source an obligation comes from and what use of its text
is permitted; maps one clause of that revision to existing requirement entries, dereferenced
through the production requirement parser; and holds an applicability decision as accepted only
when it links to an existing decision record whose body carries the digest of the exact source,
mapping, project profile and proposal, whose decision issue the named reviewer closed, with an
explicit approved disposition, a reviewer who is not the proposer, and a qualified-review
reference. Anything short of that stays unresolved. The loader reads only explicitly supplied
local inputs and never fetches the forge.

**The constraint behind it.** Applicability is an authority claim. A citation in prose cannot
reopen the right reviews when a source or a project's scope changes, and an automated
preparation result must not become a conformity or authorization claim. Keeping the contract a
library lets later review-packet work build on it while no adopting project is gated by checks
that have not been independently verified.

**What this record does not decide.** It does not activate the contract: no lint wiring, no
check that fails an adopter's obligations file, and no claim that the contract is enforced.
Activation is a separate, later decision. It does not settle whether any interpretation is
semantically right. And, per the register's own limits, it records that the alternatives were
weighed and names a human approver; whether the implementation matches the ruling is the review
gate's judgement, and the brief's Verify rows, re-run by an independent verifier, are the
change's own validation.
