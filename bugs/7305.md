# 7305 — Report the primary claim's current age

The refused-acquire diagnostic reads the standalone state and age fields from the requested key's HELD line. Reclaim notes can describe a previous holder, and an alias can carry a different age. The claim store continues to decide acquisition.

The dispatcher regression cases cover an eight-minute-old claim with a 35-minute-old note, the inverse, output prefixed by a diagnostic, and an unreadable age followed by an alias. They also assert that the refused acquisition never steals or dispatches.
