# 7305 — Report the primary claim's current age

The refused-acquire diagnostic reads the requested key's HELD line by position: state is the first `state=` word before the `note=` word, and age is the last `age=` word on the line, which the claim tool writes after the whole claim message. Reclaim notes can describe a previous holder, a steal reason can carry a tab or newline, and an alias can carry a different age; none of them supplies the claim's state or age. The claim store continues to decide acquisition.

The same rule binds every reader of a claim message: the claim tool writes a note as one word (all whitespace becomes `_`), and its field reader and the supervisor's owner reader stop at the note.

The dispatcher regression cases cover an eight-minute-old claim with a 35-minute-old note (as one word, split by a newline, and split by a tab), the inverse, a note age beside an unreadable claim age, a state found only after the note, output prefixed by a diagnostic, and an unreadable age followed by an alias. They also assert that the refused acquisition never steals or dispatches, and that the refusal names the primary HELD line. Admission occupancy counts a young claim whose note carries an old age.
